# Series Overview: Flynn's Adventure

## Premise
A children's adventure series centered on Flynn, a cute tuxedo cat about one year old, and the discoveries and experiences that unfold around Willow Pond Way.

## Series plan
- **Planned length:** 10 books
- **Main character:** Flynn
- **Primary setting:** Willow Pond Way
- **Tone:** Warm, playful, cozy, character-driven children's adventures
