# Series Overview: Flynn's Adventure

## Premise
A children's adventure series centered on Flynn, a tuxedo cat about one year old, and the discoveries and experiences that unfold around Willow Pond Way, a beautiful, forested place in Oregon.

## Series plan
- **Planned length:** 10 books
- **Main character:** Flynn
- **Primary setting:** Willow Pond Way
- **Tone:** Warm, playful, cozy, character-driven children's adventures, with room for sincere emotional moments
- **Hero's pattern:** Flynn often gets himself into trouble; other characters may help him learn from his mistakes.

## Known characters
- [Flynn](../characters/flynn.md) — hero and main character
- [Elijah](../characters/elijah.md) — two-year-old child in Flynn's family
- [Peter](../characters/peter.md) — protective, Gandalf-like peacock mentor
- [Jess](../characters/jess.md) — Flynn's mother
- [Phil](../characters/phil.md) — Flynn's father

## Planned story idea
- **"The Day Flynn Flew"** — Flynn gets stranded across a river after a log washes away. While his family misses him, Peter challenges Flynn over his irresponsibility and ultimately flies him back home. The story is an idea in development and has not yet been assigned a volume.
