# Established Canon Facts

- The series is titled **Flynn's Adventure**.
- The series is planned to contain **ten books**.
- Flynn is the central hero, a tuxedo cat about one year old.
- The primary setting is **Willow Pond Way**, a beautiful, forested place in Oregon with many rivers and creeks.
- The intended tone is warm, playful, and character-driven children's adventure, with room for sincere emotional moments.
- Elijah is a two-year-old child character who is old enough to run around outside on his own in the story world.
- Jess is Flynn's mother, and Phil is Flynn's father.
- Peter is a peacock with a Gandalf-like mentor role. He can be rough with Flynn to force him to face his mistakes, but his underlying motive is protective.
- **"The Day Flynn Flew"** is a planned story idea: Flynn crosses a river on a log, the log washes downstream, and Flynn is stranded away from his family. Elijah and Jess cry because Flynn is missing. Phil brings Elijah a new kitten, but Elijah rejects it because it feels like a replacement for Flynn. Peter scolds Flynn for being irresponsible, then helps him return home by getting him up into a tall tree and flying him across the river.
