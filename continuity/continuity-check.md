# Continuity Check

Before calling a book draft complete:

- [ ] Flynn's appearance, age, personality, and behavior match the character reference.
- [ ] Recurring characters act consistently with previous appearances.
- [ ] Locations match established descriptions and geography.
- [ ] Timeline and seasonal details make sense.
- [ ] New facts are recorded in the relevant reference file.
- [ ] Contradictions are resolved or listed in Open Questions.
- [ ] The ending preserves the series' intended tone.
