# Open Questions

Keep undecided details here so brainstorming does not accidentally become canon.

- What are Flynn's defining personality traits, habits, and favorite things beyond his tendency to get into trouble?
- What are Elijah's personality, appearance, favorite things, and speech patterns?
- What does Peter look like beyond being a peacock, and how does his unusual ability to carry Flynn through the air work?
- How does Peter know Flynn, and why has he taken on a protective mentor role?
- How should Peter's rough treatment of Flynn be portrayed so readers understand his protective intent without making the behavior seem needlessly cruel?
- What are Jess's and Phil's personalities and other roles in the family?
- What does the family's home look like, and where exactly is it on Willow Pond Way?
- What does Willow Pond itself look like, and how is it connected to the nearby rivers and creeks?
- What is the intended reading age and approximate length of each book?
- Which of the ten planned volumes will contain "The Day Flynn Flew"?
- What are the exact circumstances that cause the log to wash away, and how does Peter get Flynn into the tall tree?
- What does Phil do with the new kitten after Flynn returns, and does it appear again?
- How do animals communicate with humans in this world?
- What themes recur across the ten books?
