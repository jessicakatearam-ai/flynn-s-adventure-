# World Overview

## Premise
Flynn's stories take place in and around Willow Pond Way, a beautiful, forested place in Oregon, with rivers, creeks, and natural spaces that invite exploration and adventure.

## Tone
- Warm, playful, cozy, and character-driven, with room for real emotional stakes.
- Adventures grow from curiosity, misunderstandings, discoveries, or neighborhood events.
- Emotional stakes should be understandable to young readers and resolve with care.
- Flynn is the hero, but he can make mistakes and get into trouble. Other characters may challenge him because they care about him.

## Established world details
- Willow Pond Way is in Oregon.
- The area is foresty and has many rivers and creeks.
- The story world includes animal characters such as Flynn the cat and Peter the peacock, alongside human characters.

## Canon rule
Only explicitly established details are canon. Keep undecided possibilities in [Open Questions](../continuity/open-questions.md).
