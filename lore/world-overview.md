# World Overview

## Premise
Flynn's stories take place in and around Willow Pond Way, a neighborhood that provides a familiar home base for small mysteries, discoveries, friendships, and everyday adventures.

## Tone
- Warm, playful, cozy, and child-friendly.
- Adventures grow from curiosity, misunderstandings, discoveries, or neighborhood events.
- Emotional stakes should be understandable to young readers and resolve with care.

## Canon rule
Only explicitly established details are canon. Keep undecided possibilities in [Open Questions](../continuity/open-questions.md).
