# Flynn

## Core profile
- **Name:** Flynn
- **Species:** Cat
- **Appearance:** Tuxedo cat
- **Age:** About one year old
- **Role:** Hero and main character

## Personality and story role
- Flynn is the hero of the series and often gets himself into trouble.
- In **"The Day Flynn Flew,"** his curiosity and poor judgment lead him to cross a river on a log. The log washes downstream, leaving him stranded away from his family.
- Peter the peacock confronts Flynn about his irresponsibility, but does so because he is trying to save and protect him.
