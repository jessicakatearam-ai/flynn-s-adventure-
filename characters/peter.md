# Peter

## Core profile
- **Name:** Peter
- **Species:** Peacock
- **Role:** Mentor and rescuer; has a Gandalf-like quality

## Personality and story role
- Peter is a formidable, somewhat intimidating mentor figure.
- He can be physically rough with Flynn and grills him about acting irresponsibly.
- Peter's sternness has a protective motive: he is trying to save Flynn and make him act more wisely.
- In "The Day Flynn Flew," Peter ultimately gets Flynn up into a tall tree and flies him across the river to return home.

## Appearance and abilities
- Peacock appearance is established.
- The details of how Peter carries Flynn through the air, and the limits of this ability, remain to be developed.
