# The Day Flynn Flew

**Status:** Story concept in development  
**Series volume:** Not assigned yet  
**Setting:** Willow Pond Way, Oregon

## Core idea
Flynn crosses a river on a log. The log washes downstream, leaving him stranded on the far side of the river and unable to get back to his family. Peter the peacock, a stern and powerful mentor figure, helps Flynn return home—and Flynn's return becomes the moment when Peter's unusual ability to fly him across the river gives the story its title.

## Main characters
- **Flynn:** The hero, a tuxedo cat about one year old, whose poor judgment gets him into trouble.
- **Elijah:** Two years old and deeply attached to Flynn.
- **Jess:** Flynn's mother, who cries when he goes missing.
- **Phil:** Flynn's father, who tries to comfort Elijah by bringing home another kitten.
- **Peter:** A Gandalf-like peacock who scolds Flynn for being irresponsible but ultimately saves him.

## Story beats
1. Flynn crosses the river using a log.
2. The log washes downstream, leaving Flynn stranded on the other side.
3. Flynn cannot find a way back to his family.
4. At home, Elijah cries because Flynn is gone, and Jess cries too.
5. Phil brings home a new kitten for Elijah, but Elijah rejects the kitten because it feels like a replacement for Flynn.
6. Peter encounters Flynn and grills him about his irresponsible choice.
7. Peter's sternness comes from a desire to save Flynn and teach him to act more wisely.
8. Peter gets Flynn up into a tall tree.
9. Peter flies Flynn across the river and brings him back to his family.
10. Flynn is reunited with Elijah and his family in a warm, emotionally satisfying ending.

## Emotional center
Flynn is not interchangeable with another kitten: he has a unique bond with Elijah and his family. The separation makes that bond clear, while Peter's rescue gives Flynn a chance to learn from his mistake.

## Themes to explore
- The consequences of risky, impulsive choices
- Family attachment and missing someone you love
- The difference between replacing someone and welcoming someone new
- Stern guidance that comes from care
- Homecoming and reunion
